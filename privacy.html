Oai privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains how Napkin Group Limited ("we"), company number 10338310, registered office Park Mill, Burydell Lane, Park Street, St Albans, AL2 2EZ, United Kingdom, handles personal data in the Oai (Order.ai) mobile app on Android and iOS.

Who can use Oai

Oai is a workforce app for construction staff whose employer has given them an Oai account. There is no public sign-up.

What we process, and why

DataPurpose
Your email address, and your password when you sign inTo sign you in. Your password is checked by our servers and is not stored on your device.
Your account and role identifiersTo show the work, claims and approvals your role allows.
Price-work bookings and claims (activity, quantity, date, status and values)To record work and process approvals and payment runs.
Snag records, including descriptions, notes, questions, replies and assignmentsTo manage defects and their fixes.
Photos you take or choose for snagsEvidence for snags and fixes. Location metadata is removed when our servers process a photo, and is not kept.

Oai contains no advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking, and we do not sell personal data.

On your device

If you choose "Remember me", you stay signed in with an encrypted session credential held in your device's secure storage. Drafts and photos of unsent snags are stored encrypted on the device until they are sent or removed. None of this is included in device backups.

Where data goes

Data is sent only over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to Oai's servers, which are hosted for us by service providers (Railway for the application and database, and Cloudflare R2 for photo storage). They process it only on our instructions.

How long we keep data

  • Signing in: with "Remember me", your session lasts up to 14 days; otherwise it lasts for the working day. Signing out ends it at once. Without "Remember me", the app also removes the session from your device when it goes into the background.
  • On your device: drafts and photos of unsent snags stay only until they are sent or removed.
  • Work records: bookings, claims, approvals, payment runs and snags, with their photos, notes and history, are kept for as long as your employer uses Oai, because they form part of its work, quality and payment records. We have not set an automatic deletion period. A deleted snag is removed from view, but its record is kept; staff accounts that are no longer active are archived rather than erased.
  • If you ask us to delete your data, we will tell you what we can delete and anything we need to keep, and why.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, and you can object to or restrict some processing. Contact info@napkingroup.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Deleting your account or data

Accounts are managed by your employer. To ask for your account or data to be deleted, contact info@napkingroup.com or your employer's Oai administrator.

Changes

We will update this page if the way we handle data changes.
